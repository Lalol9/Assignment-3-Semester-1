This is a music playlist website where users can make a playlist from the songs listed on the page.
The playlist is stored locally and has it's duration and number of songs displayed in the sidebar.
You can sort the songs on screen by pressing one of the categories above the list of songs.
You can filter songs with the filter dropdown menu at the top of the screen, and search for songs with the search bar at the top of the screen.
The buttons "all songs" and "my Playlist" in the sidebar can be clicked to display a list of either all the songs available or all the songs in the user's playlist.
The playback buttons at the bottom of the screen are purely visual and have no influence over the songs listed.
